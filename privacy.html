Privacy policy and terms

Who uses it. This application is used only by its owner, for personal purposes.

What it accesses. It reads and writes files in the owner's own Google Drive, in order to keep a copy of the owner's notes there.

What it does not do. It does not collect data from any other person, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use Google user data for advertising or to train models.

Where data goes. Only between the owner's own server and the owner's own Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Terms. Provided as is, for personal use by its owner only.
